## 👋 About

**m7xlab** is a small, focused team building **approachable** tools in three areas:

| Domain | What we ship | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| 🚗 **Electric vehicles** | Telemetry, battery health & driver analytics for VW Group EVs | [**iVDrive.eu**](https://ivdrive.eu) |
| ☁️ **Developer infrastructure** | Self-hosted, opinionated platform stacks for data & AI teams | [**fast2.dev**](https://fast2.dev) |
| 🧪 **The lab itself** | Experimental libraries, internal tools, and open-source releases | This GitHub org |

We believe good data tools should feel calm, fast, and honest. No dark patterns. No lock-in. No surprises in the changelog.
